# 03 Attribution block

Rendered verbatim in the app (footer, the map attribution control and the Data page). The EOX string
is the wording EOX prescribes and must not be edited.

- Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2017 to 2026 (Sentinel-2 L2A via Earth Search on AWS Open Data).
- Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, distributed by Microsoft Planetary Computer.
- Terrain Tiles by Mapzen (Tilezen) via AWS Open Data: SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1 and regional sources (3DEP,
  Geoscience Australia CC BY 4.0, EU-DEM, CDEM, LINZ CC BY 3.0 NZ, Kartverket CC BY 4.0, Austrian DGM CC BY 3.0
  AT, INEGI, UK LIDAR OGL v3, ArcticDEM).
- Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (c) DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and (c) Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided
  under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.
- SRTM GL1 (Farr et al. 2007, doi:10.1029/2005RG000183) via OpenTopography.
- Mining polygons: Maus et al. 2022 (doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.942325, CC BY-SA 4.0); Tang and Werner 2023
  (doi:10.5281/zenodo.6806817, CC BY 4.0); Jasansky et al. 2024 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.14195737, CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Sentinel-2 cloudless - https://s2maps.eu by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024).
